About the Site

The ACM SIGMOD Blog is the official blog of ACM SIGMOD, the ACM Special Interest Group on Management of Data. It is maintained by volunteers of the data management community. If you would like to write for the blog, please contact the editors.

Code of Conduct

The data management community is committed to diversity and inclusion. We welcome people and opinions of all persuasions, as long as they abide by the ACM policy against harassment.
